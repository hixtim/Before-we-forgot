## Photographs

The site uses photographs from Wikimedia Commons. All photographs used by the website are stored locally in `assets/photo-story` and `assets/gallery`, so the pages do not need to load imagery from Wikimedia while visitors browse. The `assets/gallery` copies are display-sized downloads; creator credit, source links and licenses remain recorded below. Open each file page to review its reuse requirements before redistribution.
